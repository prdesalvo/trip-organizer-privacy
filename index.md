# Privacy Policy for Pin and Plot

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Pin and Plot is built so that your trips belong to you. The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share any of your information, and cannot see your trips.

## Our Privacy Approach

Pin and Plot has:

- No user accounts
- No custom backend
- No analytics SDK
- No advertising SDK
- No third-party trackers
- No third-party AI service

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

## Data Stored by Pin and Plot

Everything you add is stored on your device. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

- Trips, days, stays, activities, and notes
- Journeys and their legs, times, and booking details
- Saved places and personal Favorites, including optional personal names
- Packing lists and your packing library
- Expenses, cost estimates, receipts, and cost splits
- To-dos
- Your home base and preferred airport
- The people you travel with, including an optional age band

## Private iCloud Sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, Pin and Plot syncs your trips through your private iCloud database so they are available on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your synced content is stored by Apple in your iCloud account. It is not stored on a developer-operated server and is not accessible to the developer. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

## Sharing a Trip

When you share a trip, it is shared through iCloud with the people you invite, and only with them. They can see and edit that trip. Personal packing items and receipt images stay private to you. You can stop sharing or remove someone at any time. Nothing is published or made discoverable.

## Optional Visit History on iPhone

Visit recording is off by default. If you enable it for a trip and grant Always
location access, Pin and Plot uses Apple's Core Location Visits service to
record approximate stops during that trip's dates. It does not record a
continuous GPS route. Stops may be delayed or missed. Only one trip records
at a time; you can pause recording in Visit history settings.

Raw history includes approximate coordinates, arrival and departure times,
location accuracy, the associated trip and any private place labels you edit.
It stays on the recording iPhone, is excluded from device backups and is not
synced through iCloud, included in trip exports or sent to Apple Intelligence.
Raw stops expire after 90 days, with expiry enforced when the app runs. You can
delete a trip's recorded stops in the app. Raw history is cleared when the app
confirms an iCloud account change or sign-out.

When you browse recorded stops or choose a nearby place, Apple Maps receives
location information needed to resolve an address, time zone or nearby place.
Background recording itself does not look up addresses or businesses.

Only Save to Trip creates a normal saved trip place. Its notes include the
approximate visit time. That saved place follows the trip's normal sync,
sharing, export and Apple Intelligence settings. Deleting raw history does
not delete places already saved to the trip. Renaming a private stop does not
rename a place previously saved to the trip.

## Apple Intelligence and Optional Private Cloud Compute

Pin and Plot uses Apple Intelligence, when available, to read pasted text,
screenshots, photos and recordings into your trip, answer questions, create
trip overviews and summarize days. Processing runs on your device by default.

On supported devices running iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or macOS 27 or later, you can
choose **Private Cloud Compute** in the You tab's Apple Intelligence section or the trip
assistant's menu. It is off by default. Before turning it on, the app explains
what will be sent and requires you to choose Turn On. The setting applies only to the
device where you enable it; you can turn it off at any time.

When enabled, the trip assistant, trip overviews, day summaries, supported trip,
ticket and place-list imports, and requested packing suggestions can send the
content they need to Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Depending on the feature,
this includes your question and conversation; the trip's stays, journeys,
places, day plans, to-dos and notes; traveler information; your configured home
base; available weather and place-search results; or the text you import.
Packing suggestions use the trip, forecast and the saved packing items available
to its travelers, including item names, categories, tags, notes, owners, owner
notes, saved and past quantities, and usage history. Suggestions run only when
you ask, and you review them before adding items. Receipt reads, packing-list
imports and standalone stay-confirmation reads remain on device.

Apple states that data processed by Private Cloud Compute is used only to
fulfill the request, is not stored and is not accessible to Apple. The
developer cannot access it. Pin and Plot does not send this content to a
developer-operated server or another AI provider. Requests use a daily
allowance tied to your iCloud account. If Private Cloud Compute is unavailable,
the app uses on-device processing and identifies where the result was produced.

Recordings are transcribed on device. If you enable Private Cloud Compute,
the resulting text may be sent for a supported import; the recording itself
is not sent to Private Cloud Compute.

Learn more about [Apple's Private Cloud Compute privacy protections](https://www.apple.com/privacy/features/).

## Apple Services

Pin and Plot uses these Apple services to provide its features:

- **Apple Maps:** searching for places, addresses, stations, and airports; Look Around; directions. Short Apple Maps links you paste are opened with Apple to find the place they point to. When you browse optional visit history, Apple Maps also resolves recorded coordinates into addresses, time zones and nearby-place choices.
- **Apple Weather (WeatherKit):** forecasts for the places on your trip.
- **iCloud (CloudKit):** sync and sharing.
- **Private Cloud Compute:** optional Apple Intelligence processing after you enable it.

Requests to these services are handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Optional Device Permissions

Pin and Plot asks for access to device features only when you use the related feature:

- **Location:** While using the app, show where you are on maps and find nearby
  places. On iPhone, optional trip visit recording requests Always access for
  approximate background stops during the enabled trip. Recording is off by
  default; you can pause it or change location permission in Settings.
- **Camera:** Photograph tickets, confirmations, receipts, and lists.
- **Microphone and Speech Recognition:** Record what you say and turn it into text on your device.
- **Photos:** Apple's photo picker gives the app only the images you choose.
- **Contacts:** Apple's contact picker gives the app only the one card you choose when adding a traveler. The app never reads your contacts.

These permissions can be managed in your device's Settings app.

## Apple System Features

If you use Shortcuts, Siri, widgets, or Live Activities, the details needed for that feature — such as a place note or your next journey leg — are made available to that Apple system feature on your device.

## Retention and Deletion

Your trips remain on your device and in your private iCloud account until you delete them. A deleted trip goes to Recently Deleted, where you can restore it or delete it permanently. Deletions sync through iCloud.

Uninstalling Pin and Plot removes its data from that device but not from your iCloud account. To remove the iCloud copy, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Pin and Plot.

Raw visit history has a separate 90-day retention period, enforced when the
app runs, and can be deleted in Visit history settings. Places explicitly
saved from that history remain normal trip records until you delete them.

## Children's Privacy

Pin and Plot does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. A traveler's age band, if you add one, is used only to tailor packing suggestions and cost splits, and stays with your trip.
