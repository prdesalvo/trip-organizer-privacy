# Trip Organizer Support

Trip Organizer keeps a whole trip in one place — the days, the journeys between them, the places you want to see, what to pack, and what it costs — with private iCloud sync and sharing.

## Common Help

### iCloud

Trip Organizer keeps your trips in iCloud. Sign in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple Account. Once your trips have opened with a connection, they open offline too — on a plane, for example. Changes sync when you are back online, and can take a minute to arrive on another device.

### Sharing a Trip

Open the trip, tap **···** › **Trip settings** › **Share Trip**, and send the invitation. The people you invite need an Apple Account. Once they accept, the trip appears on their Trips screen and edits flow both ways. Personal packing items and receipt images stay private.

### Importing and the Trip Assistant

Reading pasted text, screenshots, photos, and recordings into a trip, and asking questions about it, use Apple Intelligence on your device. They need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in **Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri** and the model finished downloading. Everything can still be added by hand with the **+** on each screen.

### Permissions

Open the Settings app, select **Trip Organizer**, and confirm that the permission needed for the feature is enabled. Location, camera, microphone, and speech recognition are optional and requested only for related features.

### Restoring a Deleted Trip

On the Trips screen, tap **···** › **Recently deleted** and restore it.
